Why the post-ban changes
  • Fewer Yi mirrors; Jayce and LeBlanc, the worst matchups, are up. A second Sabotage hits LeBlanc's Mirror Image and Baited Hook and Jayce's Dazzling Aurora. Fiora was mirror tech, so she goes.
  • The second Alpha Strike and Decree of Strength are for LeBlanc. Decree of Strength takes a Ruined Rex from their hand.
  • For LA: 1–2 Back Off in main. Nobody expects it, the same way Not So Fast caught people in Singapore.
  • Also worth testing: Ambessa, Unyielding Spirit, Akali.

Sideboard rules 30:21

Mindset
Start with the cards that feel bad in the matchup, take those out, then fill from the side. The deck is 40 good cards, so starting from what to bring in goes nowhere. After game 1, read their build (equipment or not, etc.) and adjust.
Flex, going first
Cut 1 First Mate (you're on 2/4/6 runes and it collides with Yi and Rengar) and 1 En Garde (dead on turns 1–2 at even rune counts).
Flex, going second
Cut two 2-drops: First Mate becomes a turn-1 play, so 12 turn-1 plays is overkill. Default: 2 Lonely Poro.
Poro vs Scuttle
Against heavy removal, keep Scuttle: it dies anyway and gives hand info. When the matchup needs early might, keep Poro. Against Kennen, his turn-1 units with his legend's +2 kill Scuttle for free.

Mulligan & tempo rules 1:26:21

  • Keep a turn-1 play, a turn-2 Yi and a turn-3 play. Don't keep turn-10 cards: unlike Kennen, Yi can't filter them away.
  • Develop every turn. Avoid two units on board with an empty base.
  • Losing units for free is the main way Yi loses. Don't push a turn-1 2-drop into a board that hits 4+ might on conquer (Rengar, Diana).
  • Play around known removal: Moonfall, Cull the Weak, Thousand-Tailed Watcher, Bellows Breath, Falling Star.
  • Mulligan for Not So Fast and Defy against purple, Rengar against fair might decks, and Onslaught and Alpha Strike against hold decks (LeBlanc, Vex, Master Yi, Wuju Master).
  • Keep Zhonya's against heavy-removal decks (Kai'Sa, Jhin, Ezreal). Against Diana it's a blowout risk if they run Acceptable Losses.

Plan
  • Now the best deck in the format. You need utility, not might: cut might and removal, bring in answers.

Plan
  • Track their might from open runes. Only energy needs an open rune (power can come from recycling exhausted ones), and their legend adds +1 to every unit they play: 1 = Punch First (+5), 2 = Inferna (+4), 3 = Nidalee (+5), 5 = Rengar (+7). Also at 5: an accelerated Kai'Sa attacks for 5 on their turn (the video says 4, but Accelerate adds 1 energy).
  • Cutting 1–2 Defy is still an option but less appealing now: they run 3 Rampage + 3 Thrill of the Hunt worth countering.
  • Slight edge to Yi. You have more card draw, so play for the late game. Rengar is much stronger attacking; keep units home when they hold a lot of might.
  • Don't tap out into an early Rampage. Losing units for free is how this matchup is lost.
  • Kill Irresistible Faefolk on sight. It pulls one of your units to its battlefield. That usually makes you the defender with your legend's +2, but on Star Spring they pull you in and slam a 7-might Rengar onto you.
  • Watch for Thrill of the Hunt: units jumping battlefields, or a point stolen on your turn.
  • Decree ruling: if they Thrill the Fury unit out after you cast Decree, the Decree fizzles. Defy the Thrill.

Alternatives: Back Off, Alpha Strike, Irelia, Grim Resolve (better with Irelia), Vilemaw, Sterak's Gage (meh), Elder Dragon (meme).

Battlefields 19:27

Star Spring Game 1 / blind

Replaces the usual Seat of Power. First Mate + a 2-drop gives an early double conquer. Vs Kennen, push the 2-drop, play Yi onto Spring as a 6-might defender, and move the other unit back. Best blind pick against Aurora.

Grove of the God-Willow Going first

Few Diana or early-hold decks are left to punish a Grove opener. The card draw fixes Yi's resource problem, and in the mirror whoever controls Grove wins. Watch for Rengar ambushes on it.

Emperor's Dais Going second

Standard pick. Enables the Disarming Rake loop: conquer with the Rake, return it to hand, kill another gear next turn.

Card notes 6:04 – 19:27

Core, don't touch

  • Rengar: 6-might defender, 8 on a surprise defense. Your best attacker from hand.
  • Ruin Runner: can't be chosen by enemy spells or abilities, so Elder Dragon and most of Kennen's interaction can't touch it.
  • En Garde / Punch First / Discipline: the reason to play these colors.
  • Onslaught: +6, then +6 again from the trash via Flow, so it's effectively card draw. Won both his Swiss LeBlanc games.
  • Charm ×3: better with Kennen weaker; his Assault +2 used to cancel your legend's +2.
  • Defy: answers Jayce/Aurora ramp, Bellows Breath and Flurry of Blades.

Meta calls

  • Not So Fast (1 main): stops Switcheroo, Elder Dragon, Ruined Rex triggers and Singularity, none of which Defy can hit.
  • Alpha Strike (1 main, 1 side): effectively doubles your might for 3, at action speed, and ignores Vi's stun. Best answer to big holds. In Singapore he lost his 2nd copy on the day, played a 3rd Ruin Runner instead, and doesn't regret it.
  • Rampage over Challenge: no rune loss, and paying a power makes it +2. Kills 4–5-might units like Kennen's chosen champion with one card.
  • Sabotage: 2, even 3: more Aurora, less Kennen.

Fringe units

  • Steel Paws: 1-cost unit with deflect. Going second, a 2-drop + Paws (3 runes) threatens a turn-2 double conquer.
  • Irelia, Fervent: deflect, +1 might whenever chosen or readied, so she gets over holds. Pairs with Abandoned Hall; decent vs LeBlanc and Irelia.
  • Akali / Ambessa: 4-costs competing with Yi's turn-2 slot. They dodge Elder and Bellows and break LeBlanc holds, which makes them viable now.
  • Vilemaw: takes 10 might to get over; shuts down Rengar decks and swarm attacks.
  • Grim Resolve: +3 for 2, built to beat the mirror's +2.
  • Legion Marauder: a 3-might 2-drop once empowered. Weaker in this meta, but the LA top 8 list ran one.
  • Avoid: Astral Heron (too slow to set up for a tempo deck), Mournful Witness. Elder Dragon is a meme.

Tricks 21:25

Zhonya's off a deathknell 21:30

You keep control of a battlefield while anything is on the chain. When they kill your Poro or Scuttle, reveal Zhonya's in response to the deathknell trigger. The 2-drop still dies and its deathknell resolves (Poro draws, Scuttle reveals their hand), and Zhonya's returns to base protecting Yi instead of being spent on the 2-drop.

Ambush after the 2-drop dies 22:56

Don't commit Rengar during the combat. Let the Poro or Scuttle die: while its deathknell is on the chain the combat is still live, so play Rengar before they conquer. You get the +2 and win the fight, trading a Poro (and drawing a card) instead of Rengar.

Score on their turn 24:30

Let them conquer, then respond to their conquer trigger (Zaun Warrens, minefield) with Rengar. That starts a new combat with you attacking; Rengar outmights them, so you conquer and score on their turn. End-of-turn triggers like Annie and Targon's Peak work too.

Charm + Rengar, two ways 26:10

  • Pull in: Charm their unit onto an empty battlefield; it becomes the attacker. Rengar in on your focus makes you the defender with +2, so you win and conquer.
  • Push out: Charm their unit (e.g. Elder Dragon) off their battlefield to base and play Rengar in response. You conquer without fighting it. A close-out play at around 7 points.

Rampage as a double-duty card 28:30

Rampage buffs your unit and kills theirs at once, but leaves yours damaged. Start a combat showdown elsewhere: every unit heals when a showdown ends, so your unit comes back as a full-health attacker. Sets up hidden lethals.

Singapore run 1:41:00

Day 1

  • Pyke: free win with Decree of Focus and Ruin Runner.
  • Diana: close, won with Ruin Runner at the end. Play around Moonfall.
  • Zed: easy. Let the shadow clones conquer, then take the battlefield back. Keep Rampage for their chosen champion.
  • Fiora hybrid: lost.
  • Two LeBlancs (midrange and poke) both went late. Onslaught won them.
  • Draven: Decree of Focus is "broken".

Day 2

  • Wuju Master (XP) hold deck: lost game 1 by underestimating it, won 2–3 after respecting holds.
  • Round 11 vs Irelia: Fiora in game 3 against a protect-the-queen build.
  • Kennen: Rampage on early units starved their engine.
  • Jayce: he recommends the stream VOD as the template for playing around Aurora (Rengar on reactions, the Rake + Dais loop).

Top cut

  • Top 8 Yi mirror (vs Vilemaw + Grim Resolve tech): won as the higher seed going first. A Charm + Rengar turn blew out their going-first game, and Rengar + Zhonya's vs Yi is "GG".
  • Semis: lost to a 25-might turn-2 opener.
  • He thinks Yi is no longer the strongest deck (Jayce is) but can still top 64.
